# Reflection on AI - Assisted Debugging

## Introduction
In this task, I worked on indetifying, fixing, and documenting bugs in several Python script using AI assistance. The main goal was to evalute how effectively AI can support the debugging process and where human input

## AI Strengths
The AI was very effective in identifying common programming issues such as syntax errors, off-by-one mistakes, and incorrect logic in loops or conditions. It provided clear explanations and suggested fixes quickly, which significantly reduced debugging time. In most cases, the AI suggestions were accurate and easy to apply.

## AI Weaknesses
However, the AI sometimes generalized problems instead of identifying very specific root causes in more complex scenarios. In some cases, multiple possible fixes were suggested, requiring additional verification. It also lacked full understanding of the intended program behavior without clear context or test cases.

## Human Role
Human intervention was necessary to validate AI suggestions and ensure correctness. Testing different inputs and verifying expected outputs helped confirm whether the AI-proposed fixes were actually correct. In some cases, manual adjustments were required to refine the solution.

## Conclusion
Overall, AI significantly improved the debugging workflow by speeding up error detection and explanation. However, human judgment remains essential for validating logic and ensuring correctness. The best results were achieved when AI suggestions were combined with manual testing and critical thinking.
